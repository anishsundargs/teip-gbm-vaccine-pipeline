What TEIP does

TEIP, the Tumor Epitope Immunogenicity Pipeline, provides a computational interface for ranking candidate tumor epitopes using peptide-HLA information and tumor-context features. It is intended for early-stage research workflows in glioblastoma vaccine design and computational immuno-oncology.

Search terms and research scope

This project is relevant to glioblastoma vaccine target prediction, GBM peptide-HLA candidate prioritization, tumor epitope immunogenicity scoring, OIP5 glioblastoma antigen analysis, cancer vaccine bioinformatics, and personalized immunotherapy target ranking.

Research-use disclaimer

TEIP is not clinically validated. It does not diagnose disease, prove vaccine efficacy, or make treatment recommendations. All candidate epitopes require experimental validation.
